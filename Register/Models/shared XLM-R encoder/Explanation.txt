What can be done here (method 2)

Rather than predicting the child independently, you can make the child probability depend on its parent.

For example:

P(en∣x)=P(IN∣x)×P(en∣IN,x)

Then

                     XLM-R
                       │
                       ▼
                shared representation
                       │
              ┌────────┴────────┐
              ▼                 ▼
        P(IN | x)          P(OP | x)
              │                 │
              ▼                 ▼
       IN expert/head      OP expert/head
              │                 │
       ┌──────┼──────┐    ┌─────┼─────┐
       ▼      ▼      ▼    ▼     ▼     ▼
      en     ra     dtp  rv    ob    rs


And because:

P(en∣x)=P(IN∣x)P(en∣IN,x)

you automatically get:

P(en∣x)≤P(IN∣x)


don't need nine separate XLM-Rs

Instead:


                         XLM-R
                           │
                           ▼
                    shared representation
                           │

       IN head          OP head           IP head
